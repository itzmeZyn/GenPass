Ggenpass
Privacy

Your passwords stay on your device.

Genpass is designed as a local-first static website.

01 · HOW IT WORKS

Local generation

Passwords, passphrases and PINs are generated inside your browser using the Web Crypto API. Genpass does not require an account or a password-generation backend.

02 · STORAGE

What Genpass stores

Your selected theme is stored in your browser's local storage. The currently displayed generated password may be kept in session storage so a normal page refresh does not replace it. Generated passwords are not intentionally stored persistently by Genpass.

03 · ANALYZER

Analyzer privacy

The analyzer calculates estimates directly in your browser. Passwords entered into the analyzer are not submitted by Genpass to an external password-checking service.

04 · IMPORTANT

Use it as a utility

Genpass is a client-side utility, not a password manager. Use unique passwords for important accounts and follow the security requirements of the service you're signing into.
